# Growth Audit — Aura

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/aura-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Aura already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Aura's own design. Prepared by Adapty, based on the live iOS flow and the App Store listing.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer the €13.49 monthly plan when someone closes the paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask for the account after the purchase, not before the price | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Put their goals, their voice and their time on the paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications after the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Move the 4.7 stars, 8M+ downloads and 45k+ five-star ratings up to the plan | CR +10–15% |

Every screen in the prototype uses Aura's design. Only the order changed.

## Grounding

Prices, ratings, quotes and feature lines come from Aura's own App Store listings (US and EU storefronts), aurahealth.io and Aura's in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Aura.
